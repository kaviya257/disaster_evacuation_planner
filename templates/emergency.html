Emergency Information

Learn essential safety steps before, during, and after a disaster.

Be prepared. Stay informed. Stay safe.

Keep your phone charged, know your evacuation options, and follow instructions from official emergency authorities.

🚨 Emergency Helpline — India

112

Call 112 for immediate assistance from emergency services in India.

🌊 Flood Safety

  • Move to higher ground when flooding threatens.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from drains, rivers, and electrical wires.
  • Switch off electricity only if it is safe to do so.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions.

🌍 Earthquake Safety

  • Drop, cover, and hold on during shaking.
  • Protect your head and neck under sturdy furniture.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings and power lines.
  • Expect aftershocks and follow official guidance.

🔥 Fire Safety

  • Raise the alarm and leave using a safe exit.
  • Use stairs, not elevators.
  • Stay low under smoke if you must move through it.
  • Never re-enter a burning building.
  • Call emergency services from a safe location.

⛰️ Landslide Safety

  • Watch for official landslide warnings.
  • Leave threatened slopes when authorities advise evacuation.
  • Watch for cracks, falling rocks, or unusual ground movement.
  • Stay away from landslide debris and unstable slopes.
  • Do not cross blocked roads without official clearance.

🌀 Cyclone Safety

  • Follow weather alerts and evacuation orders.
  • Secure loose outdoor objects before conditions worsen.
  • Stay indoors, away from windows.
  • Keep away from beaches and storm-surge areas.
  • Do not go outside during a temporary lull in strong winds.

📱 General Emergency Tips

  • Share your location with trusted family members.
  • Keep emergency contacts written down.
  • Carry essential medicines and identification.
  • Use a battery-powered radio if networks fail.
  • Follow instructions from verified authorities.
