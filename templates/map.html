How to use:
  1. Select Starting Point and click the map.
  2. Select Disaster Location and click the map.
  3. Select Destination and click the map.
  4. Click Calculate Route.

The red circle represents a configurable 500-metre exclusion radius around the marked disaster.

Select a starting point on the map to begin.
Safety notice: This is a prototype. Disaster locations are manually entered, and route avoidance is only a basic estimate. Roads may be blocked, hazards may change, and the selected destination is not verified as a shelter. Always follow official emergency service instructions.
